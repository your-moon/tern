Connect

One shortcut.
Any server.

Press ⌘K and type a few letters: tern finds the host, ordered by what you used last, and connects. Groups, tags, ProxyJump and port forwards are set once per host, and a dropped network reconnects on its own.

Passwords

Passwords stay
where they are.

Logins come from your SSH key, your password manager (any command, like op read … or pass show …), or tern's encrypted vault. At a sudo prompt, press ⌘\ (Ctrl+Shift+\ on Windows and Linux) and tern types it. Never on its own.

Real work

Everything a
long session needs.

Split panes and find in scrollback, an SFTP panel to browse and drop files, snippets for the commands you run every day, broadcast input to chosen tabs, and session logs.

Memory

76 MB.
Not 580.

One native process, no browser engine. Connected to the same server, tern settles at 76 MB where Tabby takes 580. Measured with macOS footprint; the method and raw samples are in the repo.

in your own repo.

Hosts, snippets and settings sync to a private Git repository you own, encrypted with your vault passphrase before they leave your computer. No account, no subscription.
